# PeopleLens visual direction

PeopleLens should feel like a **human operations board**, not a generic SaaS dashboard.

## Design signature

- warm paper canvas, dark ink, restrained forest green and coral accents
- crisp rules and compact data rows instead of floating glass cards
- team color is semantic: it appears as a card rail, avatar accent, and capacity signal
- low-radius geometry; rounded pills are reserved for true status/control needs
- product hierarchy comes from scale, spacing, rules, and contrast before decoration
- the lens mark is the primary brand motif

## Avoid

- blue/purple AI gradients
- glassmorphism and blurred translucent panels
- neon glows
- identical rounded metric cards
- pill-shaped decoration for every label
- decorative sparkles or AI-style magic icons
- generic “modern dashboard” styling with no PeopleOps meaning

## Interaction character

- calm, direct, factual
- information-dense without feeling cramped
- color supplements labels; it never carries meaning alone
- motion stays subtle and optional
- accessibility and clarity take priority over visual novelty
